Privacy Policy

cuffly  ·  Last updated: May 23, 2026  ·  Effective date: May 23, 2026

cuffly ("the App") helps users keep blood pressure records on their device and share PDF or CSV reports when they choose to do so. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App processes, where it is stored, and when users may share it externally.

The App is not a medical device. Blood pressure entries, summaries, charts, and reports are for personal tracking only and are not intended to diagnose, treat, prevent, monitor, or replace emergency or medical decisions. Please consult a qualified healthcare professional before making health decisions.

3. Sharing and Export

The App creates PDF reports or CSV files only when the user starts the action. These files are passed to the iOS share sheet. If the user chooses an external app, service, or recipient, the file may be transmitted to that selected destination. Further handling is governed by the privacy policy of the selected external service.

4. Third-Party SDKs and Automatic Collection

The current version of the App does not include advertising, analytics, tracking, remote server sync, account login, or cloud backup features. The App does not transmit blood pressure records to developer-controlled servers.

5. Retention and Deletion

  • Profiles, blood pressure records, and App settings are stored on the user's device.
  • Users can delete individual records and profiles inside the App.
  • Deleting the App also deletes App data stored on the device.
  • Because the developer does not store this data on a server, no separate server-side deletion process is required.

6. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Security

The App stores data on the user's device and does not transmit it to developer-controlled servers. If the user shares a PDF or CSV file with another app or service, the security and handling of that file are governed by the selected external service.

8. Changes to This Policy

This Policy may be updated to reflect changes in App features, laws, regulations, or store policies. If there are material changes, the "Last updated" date on this page will be updated.
