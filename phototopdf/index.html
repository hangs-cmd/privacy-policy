Privacy Policy

Photo to PDF – Image Converter · Last updated: October 1, 2026

Photo to PDF – Image Converter ("the App") turns photos into PDF files. The App has no account and no server of its own. This policy explains what stays on your device and what third-party services process.

1. Your photos and documents stay on your device

The photos you choose, the PDF files you create, and their preview images are processed and stored only on your device. The App never uploads them. PDF conversion works without a network connection.

PDF files are saved to your device's Downloads folder. They remain there until you delete them, including after you uninstall the App.

2. Information the App does not collect

The App does not ask you to provide an account, name, email address, phone number, contacts, or location. Your documents and their recognized text may contain personal information; this content stays on your device. The App does not request access to your whole photo library. You choose photos through the Android photo picker or take one with the system camera.

The App contains no analytics or crash-reporting service.

3. Information stored on your device

The App keeps the following on your device only:

  • The list of documents you created (file name, location in Downloads, page count, size, compression choice, date)
  • Categories, tags, and text recognized from document pages for local search and previews. Text recognition and any available AI name suggestions run on your device; document content is not sent to an AI service
  • Whether "Remove ads" was purchased
  • Usage counts and times, such as how many PDFs were created, when the App was first opened, and at which step the last failed attempt stopped. These contain no file names, no file paths, and no document content. They leave your device only if you choose to share them yourself

This information is deleted when you uninstall the App or clear its data. The App excludes its private files and database from Android backup and device transfer. Separately saved PDFs in Downloads are subject to your own backup and sharing choices.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App shows full-screen ads served by Google AdMob. Ads are requested over the network. To serve and measure ads, and to prevent fraud, Google's advertising SDK may collect and process:

  • Your IP address, which may be used to estimate your general location
  • Device and advertising identifiers, such as the Android advertising ID and app set ID
  • Interactions with the App and with ads, such as app launches and taps
  • Diagnostic information, such as app launch time and performance data

This data is sent to Google encrypted in transit. The App does not receive or store it.

Where required, for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland, a consent form is shown before ads are requested. You can review your choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy options. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.

Purchasing "Remove ads" turns off all advertising in the App.

See Google's Privacy Policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy and ad settings at https://adssettings.google.com.

5. Purchases

The optional one-time "Remove ads" purchase is processed by Google Play. The App never sees or stores your payment details. The App only learns whether the purchase exists.

6. Sharing

When you tap Share, the PDF is handed to the app you choose through the Android share sheet. What happens next is governed by that app.

7. Third-party services

If you choose to download the optional AI model, the App connects to Hugging Face and its download infrastructure to retrieve the Unsloth model file. These servers receive connection information such as your IP address. Your document text is not included in the request. The downloaded model stays on your device and can be deleted in Settings → Model management. Suggestions run locally and categories and tags are saved only when you apply them.

  • Google AdMob and Google User Messaging Platform (advertising and consent)
  • Google Play Billing (purchases)

Each is governed by its own privacy policy.

8. Data retention and deletion

The App has no server-side database and cannot access, restore, or delete your data remotely. Uninstalling the App removes the data described in section 3. PDF files in your Downloads folder are yours to keep or delete.

Data processed by Google for advertising is retained under Google's policies.

9. Children

The App is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.
