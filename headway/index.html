Headway — Privacy Policy

App: Headway (com.dotlinerlab.headway) · Developer: Dotliner Lab · Last updated: 2026-08-03

Headway is a minimalist arrow puzzle game. We keep data collection to a minimum. This policy explains what is and isn't collected.

1. What we collect

We (Dotliner Lab) do not collect any personal data. Headway has no account, no login, and no server of our own. Your game progress and settings are stored only on your device.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Headway shows ads through Google AdMob: banner, interstitial, and optional rewarded ads. Rewarded ads only play when you choose to watch them to earn in-game boosters. To serve ads, Google and its partners may collect and process certain data, such as your device's advertising identifier, approximate (non-precise) location, IP address, and app interactions. This is governed by Google's policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses data (AdMob)

On first launch, Headway shows a consent screen (Google UMP) where you can accept or decline personalized ads as required in your region. You can change this anytime in the consent options. Declining still lets you play the full game.

3. Children

Headway is a general-audience puzzle game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

4. Data sharing & sale

We do not sell your data. We do not share data with anyone except as required for the advertising described above (Google AdMob).

5. Your choices

You can reset all local data by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling. You can manage ad personalization via the in-app consent options or your device's ad settings.

6. Contact

Questions? Contact us at dotlinerlab@gmail.com.
