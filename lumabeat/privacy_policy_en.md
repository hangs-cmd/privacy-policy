# LumaBeat Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 8, 2026

LumaBeat (the "App") provides wellness reference metrics such as heart rate, HRV, and signal quality using a smartphone camera. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App processes, how it is used, and how it is protected.

The App is not a medical device. Measurement results are not intended to diagnose, treat, prevent, or monitor any disease or medical condition, and they should not be used for emergency decisions. If you have health concerns or are worried about your results, please consult a qualified healthcare professional.

## 1. Information We Process

LumaBeat may process the following information to provide App features.

### Camera Data

The App uses the front camera to analyze subtle facial color changes in real time. The App does not save camera videos, photos, or face images. Camera input is processed on the device to calculate wellness reference metrics.

### Health and Wellness Measurement Data

The App may store the following measurement data on your device to show your history and charts:

- Heart rate (BPM)
- HRV-related metrics
- Signal Quality Index (SQI)
- Measurement time
- Analysis data such as PPG waveform, IBI, and FFT spectrum
- Lighting profile and measurement quality information

This data is not a medical record. It is used to display reference history and charts within the App.

### Settings and App Preference Data

The App may store settings required for App functionality, such as language preference, onboarding completion, notification settings, and cloud sync consent.

### Advertising and Third-Party SDK Data

The App may use third-party SDKs such as Google AdMob, Google User Messaging Platform, and Firebase to provide advertising, consent management, app configuration, and related services. These SDKs may process information such as device identifiers, advertising identifiers, approximate location, ad interaction data, and diagnostic information according to their own policies.

The App does not sell or share health measurement data for personalized advertising purposes.

## 2. How We Use Information

LumaBeat uses information for the following purposes:

- To provide camera-based wellness measurement features
- To calculate heart rate, HRV, signal quality, and related results
- To show measurement history, charts, and detailed analysis
- To save App settings
- To sync measurement data to the cloud only when you explicitly enable cloud sync
- To show ads and manage privacy choices
- To improve App stability and manage remote configuration

## 3. Cloud Sync

By default, LumaBeat stores health measurement data on your device.

Only if you enable "Health Data Cloud Sync" in the App settings, some measurement results may be sent to Firebase Firestore. Cloud sync is optional, and you can turn it off at any time in the App settings.

If you turn off cloud sync, future measurement data will not be sent to the cloud. To request deletion of data that has already been synced, please contact us using the contact details below.

## 4. Sharing of Information

LumaBeat does not sell your personal information. Information may be shared or processed in the following cases:

- When you use a sharing feature to export data to another app
- When service providers such as Firebase or AdMob process data needed to provide App features
- When required by law or valid legal process
- When necessary to protect the rights, safety, or security of users or others

## 5. Retention and Deletion

Measurement data stored on your device may be deleted when you delete App data or uninstall the App. If the App provides an in-app deletion feature, you may delete measurement history directly.

To request deletion of cloud-synced data, please contact us using the details below. We may need additional information to verify your request or identify the relevant data.

## 6. Security

LumaBeat uses reasonable technical and organizational measures to help protect user data, including on-device storage, Firebase security features, and security measures provided by third-party SDK providers. However, no method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is completely secure.

## 7. Children's Privacy

LumaBeat is not directed to children. If we become aware that we have processed personal information from a child where legal protections apply, we will take appropriate steps after verification.

## 8. Third-Party Services

The App may use the following third-party services:

- Google Firebase
- Google AdMob
- Google User Messaging Platform

The privacy practices of these services are governed by their respective policies.

This Privacy Policy may be updated to reflect changes in App features, laws, regulations, or store policies. If there are material changes, we will notify users through an in-app notice or by updating this policy.
