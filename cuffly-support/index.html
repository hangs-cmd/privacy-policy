cuffly Support

Blood pressure tracking support page

cuffly helps you keep blood pressure readings on your device, review trends, set local reminders, and share PDF or CSV summaries when you choose.

Important Health Notice

cuffly is not a medical device. It does not measure blood pressure, diagnose medical conditions, provide treatment recommendations, or replace advice from a qualified clinician. If you have health concerns or unusually high or low readings, contact a healthcare professional.

Frequently Asked Questions

  • Where is my data stored? Profiles, blood pressure records, and reminder settings are stored on your device.
  • Does cuffly sync data to a server? No. The current version does not include account login, cloud sync, analytics, ads, or developer-controlled server transmission.
  • How can I delete my data? You can delete readings and profiles inside the app. Deleting the app also removes app data stored on your device.
  • How does sharing work? PDF and CSV files are created only when you choose to share or export them through the iOS share sheet.
  • Why does the app request notifications? Notifications are used only for optional local morning and evening measurement reminders.
